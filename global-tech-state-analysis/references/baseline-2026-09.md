## Regime notes

- Industrial training on HBM accelerators; largest observed sites grew toward mid-2026 reports of several hundred thousand accelerator-equivalents. Mid-2026 flagships often omit token and FLOP figures.
- Jagged frontier: contest-math and coding benches near the top coexist with brittle perception and ~1/3 agent failures on OSWorld-class tasks.
- NVIDIA still dominates frontier training; Vera Rubin entered production shipment in 2H 2026; AMD MI350-class is competitive on some inference slices; TPU v7 is cloud-walled.
- QEC is experimental fact (Willow-style distance scaling; ion-trap logical-error reduction with post-selection; large encoded circuits). Utility remains narrow.
- Reversible / adiabatic: Vaire CMOS resonator prototypes with partial energy recovery; superconducting shift registers below Landauer per bit-shift because information is not erased; NSA-assigned fluxon patents in the public record. None of this is a training cluster.

## Toward 50 vs not toward 100

Toward 50: below-Landauer information-preserving shifts; partial adiabatic CMOS recovery; optical rack interconnect (movement joules, not gate joules). None yet punch through 35 overall.

Not toward 100: any claim that LLMs, logical qubits, or fluxon logic traverse dimensions and time (G4).
