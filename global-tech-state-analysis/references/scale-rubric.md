# GEM Techno-Scale Rubric

Scores measure physical and engineering regime, not cultural salience.

## Anchors

| Score | Regime |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Electrical signaling and storage of information begins. |
| 10 | Reliable vacuum-tube / relay digital machines; stored-program concept exists. |
| 20 | Transistorized von Neumann machines; digital computing is industrial. |
| 30 | CMOS + networking + software abstraction at planetary scale; GPUs as tensor engines; ML is an industrial method. |
| 35 | Frontier AI is economically material; clusters at 1e5-1e6 accelerator class; early below-threshold QEC; lab-scale energy-recovery logic. |
| 40 | Production energy-recycled or cryogenic reversible subsystems co-located with AI factories; logical qubits used as scarce scientific instruments. |
| 50 | Practical superfluid / near-unlimited post-expenditure energy for computation. Dissipation is no longer the binding constraint. |
| 70 | Fielded fully reversible machines at datacenter scale. |
| 100 | Effectively lossless reversible computation; higher-frequency / quantum effects used to sidestep classical limits including dimensional / temporal traversal. Treat 80-100 as G4 unless primary evidence appears. |

## Sub-score guidance

AI / LLMs — 35 production multimodal agents with jagged superhuman benches; 45 stable on-the-job self-improvement with verifiable rewards; 50+ not granted for intelligence alone.

GPUs / tensor — 30 CUDA-class + HBM; 38 rack-scale NVLink / TPU pods + FP4/FP8; 45 production optical or superconducting fabric that removes the energy wall; 50 recycled energy as the training default.

Quantum — 15 NISQ; 22 below-threshold QEC and O(10-100) logical qubits; 35 utility-scale logical machines on a fair cost basis; 50 thermodynamically cheap quantum factories.

Core CS — 28 mature compilers / distributed systems / ML theory; 35 verified agent stacks and practical reversible ISAs; 50 models that assume recycled energy or new physical oracles.

## Anti-compression rule

Do not score 45+ on the parent scale because models feel almost AGI. Irreversible 700-1400 W GPUs keep the civilization in the 30s.

## Movement rules

+1 to +3 for a new production regime. +4 to +8 for a change of physical substrate in production. G4 claims do not move the score.
